# YCA1 bare-field convention and independent assembly target

Strip a common 1/(16*pi^2*epsilon). Let W denote the measured FYS1 family
matrix, k_R=Tr(Y_R^dagger Y_R) the FKP1 scalar trace, and h_R the scalar
kinetic metric on the R carrier. Their values must be read from measured
tables, not assigned from a desired Ward identity. Inherited residues give
Z_F,Y=-W/2 and Z_s,Y=-k_R h_R. No additional family multiplicity applies.

The bare density at first order is

    (1/2) F^T [ M + deltaM
       +(Z_F^T M+M Z_F)/2 + B(Z_s x)/2 ] F.

There is no extra Pfaffian half on either external leg; the density's half
is common to its tree and counterterm vertex. The transpose on the first
fermion field is a congruence, not a Hermitian similarity transformation.
The conjugate density uses the conjugated expression. Parameter variation
is fixed by minus the independently measured proper-vertex pole and minus
this field image. YPP1 supplies the complete zero proper Y^3 pole; it does
not set the external-field contribution or parameter pole to zero.

Gauge-Yukawa terms reuse the separately qualified GYV1 image. Its independent
description is -3/2(C_I^T M+M C_I), where C_I=sum_a T_a T_a. The two gauge
widths cancel only after both oriented scalar diagrams and field terms
are included. No new gauge-width projection is performed here.

For constant U with U^dagger U=I, the family convention is Y'=U^T Y U.
Then Y'^dagger Y'=U^dagger Y^dagger Y U, using U* U^T=I; its trace is
unchanged. A field image involving W^T Y+Y W transforms by U^T (...) U.
This proves the general covariance law once the actual assembled expression
has that form. Exact finite unitary tests are sensitive diagnostics of its
implementation, not proofs based on sampling alone. A common phase has
the same law; matrix polynomials retain real and imaginary coordinates.

The full field-space closure follows from the inherited FYS tensor identity
W tensor I16, the FKP diagonal metrics and zero cross metric, and actual
B_R h_S=(delta_RS/2)B_R. Verify every internal/scalar position before using
this factorization for the family tensor. There are18 family polynomials,
167936 two-carrier internal/scalar positions and1511424 family/internal/
scalar slots. They are algebraic positions, not independent experiments.

This is a source-free barred UV parameter image. Multiplying a mass spurion
by this answer does not define a VEV, tadpole or gauge-fixing mass/source
subtraction. Likewise a barred kinetic field renormalization acts on the
finite-hat mixing and sources if extended to the full action; those partner
terms are not supplied by this calculation. No finite prescription adopted.
