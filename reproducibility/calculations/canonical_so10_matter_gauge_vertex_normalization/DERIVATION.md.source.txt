# MGN1 fixed old B image and Nambu/cotangent field normalization

The fixed old primitive contains, in32pi²eps units,
K_B=g² JB (DB_raw B+ZA_raw A). B is even, so its canonical field direction
is Y_B=-DB_raw B-ZA_raw A. For the matter inverse operator -ig gamma T.B,
its NEGATIVE variation has the pole-image currents, in16pi²eps units,

    L_B,old,c=-i/2 sum_d (Q DB_raw Q^-1)_dc T_d,
    L_A,old,h=-i/2 sum_d (Q ZA_raw RH)_dh T_d,
    Q_H RH=H_new, R=-L^T.

Both the even canonical sign and the pole-unit1/2 must be retained. These
matrices are extracted from the actual frozen primitive, not assigned from
the measured fermion/gauge result or a guessed Casimir formula.

Let the measured kinetic matrix coefficients be C_g=C_width,
C_10=(5/16)I16,C_126=(63/16)I16, lifted with their full family products.
The fixed Hermitian field direction is F_L=-C/2,F_R=-C^T/2, inverse source
K->K(1-tF). In Nambu notation K_Pform=C_Lorentz D, the field congruence is
D->C_Lorentz^-1(1+tF)^T C_Lorentz D(1+tF). The qualified charge transpose
preserves each chirality while barred gamma swaps it. Thus the negative
vertex variation has

    L_field,c=i(F_R^T T_c+T_c F_L)=-i{C,T_c}/2,
    R_field,c=-(L_field,c)^T.

The negative kinetic image is -(F_L+F_R^T)=C. The source-pole image is
-i[T_c,F_L]=+i[T_c,C]/2, with right image the PLUS transpose. This distinct
source transpose sign must not be confused with the ordinary vertex sign.
Yukawa field matrices commute with all internal generators, so their source
commutator image is zero. The nonzero gauge image must match MFI1, already
used by MSU1; no new qK_P transformation is introduced.

Compare the actual sum of all five loop families with the sum of these fixed
images, grade by grade. Pure-gauge PGV1 suggests a possible equality
NB=(Q DB_raw Q^-1)/2, but that is an unfitted hypothesis until extraction
and exact comparison. Include old ZA and independent A. Preserve all full45
matrix/nonadjoint terms, zeros and correlated source/kinetic projections.
