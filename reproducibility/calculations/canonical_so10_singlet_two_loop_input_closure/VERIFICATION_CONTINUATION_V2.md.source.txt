coefficient. The dependent light-Higgs mass contains a nested radical outside
its multiquadratic parser. That coefficient has zero S-background derivative.

derivative it first computes the EXACT integer S degree after both external

parsing an irrelevant mass constant. Every nonzero derivative coefficient,
all raw_D8 matrices, all D positions/zeros, metrics and scientific checks
remain. This is not setting a physical mass or propagator to zero: no mass
operator/integral is being calculated here. General algebraic inverse and
nested-root infrastructure would add no verification value to this derivative.
