## 1. Read the original requirements at their actual quantifiers

The domain contract separates closure from adequacy. The literal basic space
still omits the original nonhorizontal ghost-rescaling image. Neither exactness
nor later success erases that witness. An explicitly justified replacement
policy and comparison are required instead of pretending literal inclusion.

W2 describes a conditional prescription class, including A1-A5; it does not
prove nonempty realization in every required sector. W3 writes the equations
through order four. Its ONE-loop equation is Q Gamma1=0. Its next equation is
Q Gamma2+(Gamma1,Gamma1)/2=0. W6 states necessary conditions on a first local
difference of restored constructions. It neither makes every closed algebraic

Thus ESP1 can constrain the specified restored-through-hbar2 endpoints. It
cannot be used to remove unweighted classes from a purely first-order cocycle
space. W3's display of higher-order equations is not their proof, and a relative
comparison does not construct the common baseline Gamma2. No new free-limit
normalization or narrower physical ring is adopted here.
