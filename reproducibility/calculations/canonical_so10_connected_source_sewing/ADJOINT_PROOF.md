# What the adjoint reconciliation establishes

The actual H-ghost/vector part of qB has 406 nonzero coefficient entries,
with identical color action in all four Lorentz components. It preserves
the declared H/M split. The 334 auxiliary entries satisfy exactly

    beta_h = P_M A_h P_M,

where A_h is the color action extracted from qB, with its inherited sign.
The FJB operation and original source-action coefficients reproduce the
same tensors. No opposite-sign fit or average over mass/charge sectors
is used. Removing the common single g10 monomial here identifies the
representation matrix; it does not allow inverse-g10 counterterms.

The common left invariant space of A_h is two-dimensional. A basis in
the actual 45-coordinate chart is

    (sqrt(30)/10)*e0 + (sqrt(2)/2)*e5 + e8,   e12.

It is contained in the 13-dimensional annihilator tested in CNS1A. This
also explains why calling that larger space the full H-invariant module
was incorrect: it omitted the unbroken adjoint constraints.

For both actual generators, projection of

    K_D F_D^{-1} F_BB-K_BB

on these two covectors is zero. Thus IF an auxiliary tadpole transforms
in this adjoint module and satisfies the corresponding renormalized
background condition, this candidate BB response is annihilated for
any values of its two invariant tadpole coefficients. No normalization
constant needs to be chosen for this algebraic cancellation.

The antecedent is consequential. This increment has not constructed the
actual tadpole, proved its renormalized background/source condition or
derived the complete y_ij from a regulated stationary map. It has not
proved that this candidate polynomial is the entire tadpole response.
In particular, the fixed heat regulator and the proper power subtractions
must be transported through the same tree map. The algebraic inclusion
does not remove the delta/IR and finite-part diagnostics in PROOF.

The least expensive next test is the full even-source-plane canonical
reduction of the original action, including its boundary current and
source transformation. It should derive the complete mean response,
check which ordinary/source Ward conditions constrain the tadpole, and
compare the fixed-heat tree map after the actual proper subtractions.
An exact remaining contact or missing condition is retained if present.
