# MSU1 fixed images and independent reconstruction

Old generator components are K_lead=g10 JC L1 C and K_ordinary=g10² JC L2 C,
with L1 including xi and L2 linear in eta_H,xi. They are not chosen here.
Their exact old scalar/gauge/source normalization convention is32pi²eps.
The measured matter loop convention is16pi²eps. For either fixed matrix,
the negative source-coordinate variation gives new left pole image

    G_c=-i/2 sum_a (Q Lraw Q^-1)_ac T_a,

with right image -G_c^T. The1/2 converts units; it is not a Nambu half.
Leading and ordinary ghost matrices contribute at g10² and g10³ respectively.

The independent old ordinary formula is

    L2_plane=-eta_H C_H,adj -xi P_M(C_A,adj-C_H,adj)P_M,
    L1_plane=xi P_M(C_A,adj-2C_H,adj)P_M.

Use C_A,adj=-sum_a f_a²=8I, C_H,adj=-sum_h f_h² with the actual H_new frame.
Do not treat the older H-adapted frame as the same45-plane coordinates.
Primary literal extraction and replay Lie construction must agree with the
actual Q bridge, including zeros and all off-diagonal entries.

At g10³ add the fixed MFI1 image E_L=i/2[T_c,C_width] and E_R=E_L^T,
obtained from the measured kinetic half and its cotangent source inverse.
Thus test Gamma_aux against G_lead, and Gamma_ordinary against G_ordinary+E.
No coefficient is inferred from either measured Gamma. The leading xi is
included in this comparison; MAS1's stored tensor factors it outside.

If these equations pass, the required local source counterterm projection
is minus this measured sum. That does not establish the validity of a full
regulated canonical change, its Jacobian, its hatted source-free companion or
a finite normalization. In particular SMA1's external qK_P=0 is unchanged.
