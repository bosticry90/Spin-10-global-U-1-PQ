# S4A1 model-specific application and original forest ownership

The complete S4F lambda[A,B] functional is valid on the invariant covariance
module. S4P justifies replacing each single background probe square by its
ideal Casimir average AFTER contraction. Within each SM irrep Cbar is
central; multiplicity matrices still mix and are retained. Distinct SM
representations sharing a mass are never charge-averaged together.

M=Htree+g10²*G on328 canonical real fields, G=O O.T at the inherited
reference width1. The qualified physical/soft/Goldstone projectors are
orthogonal and complete, of total ranks290,5,8,1,12,12. The exact light
projector is P0; positive projector P+=I-P0 has rank323. Htree annihilates
all Goldstones and P0, and is positive on the290 physical directions.
Every positive eigenvalue, however small, is included. No adopted physical
Goldstone mass is implied by this reference operator.

Let R=M^+, A=M*(1+log(Q2)-log(M)) on P+. The matched finite result
is Lambda=-lambda[A,Cbar R]/4. The common scale convention Q2=60 exp(L)
gives Lambda=C(g10²)+L*S(g10²), with
S=-lambda[M,Cbar R]/4. There is NO compulsory additional g10² prefactor.
This differs from vector primitive formats because quartic scalar couplings
are already dimensionless after the external background coupling cancels.

For an explicit common light mass delta>0 set
A_delta=A+delta*(1+log(Q2/delta))*P0,
B_delta=Cbar R+Cbar P0/delta.
Expanding the same actual source functional gives four exact channels.
The heavy/light term -lambda[A,Cbar P0]/(4delta) equals the opened heavy
mass-insertion graph. The all-light term
-lambda[P0,Cbar P0]*(1+log(Q2/delta))/4 equals the EFT quartic graph.
After their subtraction the difference from the hard result is precisely
-delta*(1+log(Q2/delta))*lambda[P0,Cbar R]/4, which vanishes. Keep the
charged light Higgs channel; its zero-mass limit is not a deletion rule.

The original class forest remains, before any light limit,
Cfull= lambda[M_delta,Cbar*H_eps(M_delta)/M_delta]/(4eps),
where M_delta=M+delta P0 and H_eps(m)=exp(gammaEeps)Gamma(1+eps)(Q2/m)^eps.
Its soft part closes on P0; Chard=Cfull-Csoft closes on P+. The tadpole
mass argument M_delta is retained in BOTH pieces. Use Cparent-Cfull once
in final assembly, not Cparent on top of an already subtracted primitive.
The S4K action-derived proper-forest identity leaves no remaining overall
pole for this family; that is not an overall CT for the other families.

The exact source functional and spectral matrix functions define the
complete amplitude for g10²>0 in this fixed reference. Two numerical
diagnostics establish implementation/convergence, not a uniform error
bound for all couplings and not physical gauge unification. Physical
finite mass, field, parameter, tadpole and reference normalizations and
the remaining SSS input/final parent assembly stay separate obligations.
