# FCT1 derivation and scope

## Coordinate covariance

For a constant even change q=T y, Z'=T^t Z T, H'=T^t H T and
G'=T^-1 G T^-t. Every vertex leg is covariant and every internal line is
contravariant. Each contracted pair supplies T T^-1=1; sources transform
cotangently. The same argument applies independently to each parity block;
the closed ghost sign/supertrace is unchanged by an even similarity. Thus the
three quartic and six cubic two-loop skeleton families, every local forest
insertion and every soft/EFT contraction are scalar coordinate invariants.
Pole/finite extraction, angular integration and mass Taylor operations are
linear and commute with constant T. No momentum-dependent redefinition or
finite pseudodifferential determinant multiplicativity is asserted.

The exact heavy bridge transports M'=T_r^t M T_r, t'=T_r^t t,
R'=R T_r, giving R'M'^-1t'=RM^-1t. Constant Berezinian affects only the
removed field-independent vacuum normalization. When complexifying real
coordinates the real structure must also be transported; physical reality is
inherited, not replaced by a new complex theory.

An internal Hermitian charge Gram transforms B'=T^dagger B T. A polynomial
F² coefficient transforms N'=T^t N T. Confusing these operations is an error.
For each simple ideal projector C_i of rank d_i the finite coefficient is
Tr(C_i Z^-1 N)/d_i; after transport C_i'=T^-1 C_i T this trace is unchanged.
FAN's complete 12x12 matrices at both diagnostic couplings and both L
coefficients are tested with a complex nonorthogonal T mixing ideal coordinate
axes while transporting C_i. This does not mix physical gauge factors.
The full charge Grams and actual HTB inverse-Hessian map are also checked.
Wrong transpose and unchanged metric must fail; diagonal-only probes do not
suffice. Structural covariance, rather than printed matrix equality, covers
the general nine-family contraction and nonlinear BMC conversion.

## Positive heavy-mass coincidences

Before a spectral decomposition, every Euclidean reference edge is a matrix
resolvent. On a compact positive heavy gap, its mass derivatives have uniform
denominator bounds. At fixed common light regulator delta>0, the forest-
subtracted parameter integrals and local subtraction coefficients are smooth
in heavy masses through equality. In a Schwinger sector the heavy exponential
has a common strictly positive lower bound; UV-subtracted sector remainders
are integrable uniformly on that compact set. Differentiation only improves
large-momentum denominators, and the finite local subtraction polynomials
and logs are smooth there. Evanescent coefficients times poles must remain.

In sectors with light lines use the original common full/EFT subtraction
before removing delta. SRM's fixed-gap hard Wilson expansion contracts all
soft subgraphs to the actual EFT including higher Wilson terms. Heavy
resolvents in these coefficients retain that same compact bound. Consequently
the subtracted hard coefficient is smooth in positive heavy coincidences;
this does not interchange an unmatched massless limit with a momentum limit.
No new proof of gap closure, changed light spectrum, Lorentzian threshold or
uniform bound at g=0/infinity is claimed.

Apparent 1/(x-y) singularities from diagonalization/reduction are divided
differences of these smooth functions: f[x,y]=integral_0^1 f'(y+u(x-y))du.
They tend to f'(x). Higher differences have the simplex derivative formula.
At a degenerate eigenspace, sum complete projectors first; its charge operator
is retained. Equal masses do not permit replacing distinct charges by an
average. The pre-decomposition contraction proof covers all nine families and
the parent-minus-family forest, not just the scalar diagnostic below.

The sunset is continuous across its positive-mass Kallen pseudothreshold;
