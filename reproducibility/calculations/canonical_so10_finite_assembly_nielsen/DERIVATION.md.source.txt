## Source construction before the auxiliary Gaussian

(zero Nielsen-source momentum), and the actual gauge fermion

-chi1*barc*r/2, the second remains chi2*barc*s/2. Equating widths BEFORE
differentiating loses this distinction. The source K_eta*c*v has only one
