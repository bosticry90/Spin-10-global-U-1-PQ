\sect{Abstract}
This note specifies a nonsupersymmetric Spin(10) gauge/scalar construction
with global PQ selection rules. Failed earlier scalar points and a distinct
classical feasibility witness are retained separately. The latter has a
large local-inclusive one-loop singlet correction despite smaller nonlocal
momentum diagnostics. All nine retained bosonic two-loop primitive families,
their prescribed finite assembly and selected coupling evaluations are now

Symbolic matter UV normalizations and a selected parity-even closed-fermion
finite comparison have also advanced. Complete massive matter matching,
physical unification, quantum control and viability remain unresolved.
All results retain their point, reference, loop-order and domain limits;
publication makes no new scientific acceptance decision.
